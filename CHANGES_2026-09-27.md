# 27 Sep 2026: the facial suture (schema 6.2)

The first of the guide's classifying characters the generator can carry. Gon (2009) p. 21: proparian, gonatoparian and
opisthoparian sutures differ only in where the posterior branch meets the cephalic margin.

## anatomy/head.py (the only anatomy touched)
* suture_path(P, S): the right-side polyline from the plan's own functions: anterior margin beside the glabella -> the
  eye, a loop round the eye's axial side (1.15 eye radii, clear of the eye's rim trough and of the glabella) -> the margin.
  A blind head has no loop (Trimerocephalus). plan() returns it as S["suture"] for the blueprint to draw.
* zfun: the groove is the LAST term, after every floor (rear band, hood, occipital ring), so nothing fills it back in:
  z -= sutureDepth * exp(-d^2 / 2 sigma^2), sigma 0.35 mm, d = distance to the polyline (_polyline_dist, vectorised).
* sutureDepth = 0 skips the term entirely: the plan is bit-identical to 6.1. Frozen references, test_head, and the
  three frozen readings (proetida 22.81, corynexochida 22.66, harpetida 30.39) pass unchanged. No reading can move
  unless a preset sets sutureDepth > 0, which none does.

## schema.py
* sutureEnd  [-1, 1]  -1 lateral margin forward of the genal angle (proparian, Phacopina), 0 the genal angle
                      (gonatoparian, Calymenina), +1 the posterior margin inboard of it (opisthoparian, Ptychopariina)
* sutureDepth [0, 0.6] mm, default 0. Both in cell a1's "more" list. 98 -> 100 parameters. SCHEMA_VERSION 6.2.

## tests/test_suture.py (head module only; nothing here builds a joint or an animal)
depth 0 is bit-identical for every sutureEnd; the plan carries the path; the groove is the full depth on the path and
absent 2 mm off it and on the axis; mirror-symmetric; each sutureEnd lands on the margin it names; the loop stays
outside the eye's rim and off the axis; a blind head's suture is straight; the shell still builds closed and loses
under 2 % volume. tests/test_schema.py: version string 6.1 -> 6.2.

## not done
blueprint.py does not yet draw S["suture"] on the PLAN view (a dashed line, as Gon draws it); web/index.html has no
slider for it beyond the a1 "more" list. The tree page's "proparian sutures" tile can now map to sutureEnd.


# 27 Sep 2026 (later): eye ridges, and fine detail through the builder's blur

## anatomy/head.py
* eye_ridge_path(P, S): axial furrow beside the glabella's frontal lobe (0.74 Lc from the rear) -> the anterior end of
  the palpebral lobe. Straight; sigma 0.6 mm; raised by eyeRidge mm. The primitive character (Gon p. 41, Redlichia);
  0 = the derived state and the default. A blind head keeps a ridge (some atheloptic forms do). S["eye_ridge"].
* the plan now returns zfun_base, detail and zfun = zfun_base + detail. detail = ridge - suture groove.

## mesh.py: sample_grid / heightfield_shell / under_envelope take detail=None
Found while testing the ridge: sample_grid's 3-tap blur is scaled to a fixed smoothing LENGTH (~1.25 mm sigma at the
161-wide grid; it is the BREP builder's, kept so a finer grid does not change shape). Any feature narrower than that
builds damped: the 0.6 mm ridge came out at 43 % height, and the suture groove (sigma 0.35) would have built at ~27 %
(a 0.4 mm suture printing 0.1 mm deep). Fix: `detail` is added AFTER the blur. head.shell / solid / cells pass the
plan's detail; a head without ridge or suture passes None and takes the old path bit for bit. Checked: test_head's
frozen heads, test_mesh, test_regression and the three frozen readings all pass unchanged.

## schema.py: eyeRidge [0, 1.5] mm, default 0, in cell a1's "more". 101 parameters.

## tests
tests/test_eye_ridge.py (head module only, 8): height 0 bit-identical; on-line height, off-line zero, axis untouched;
mirror-symmetric; runs from beside the glabella, inboard of the eye, forward-to-back, ending at the eye's front edge;
a blind head keeps one; ridge and suture coexist at their own heights; the built skin (same grid, vertex for vertex)
rises by the full height on the line and nowhere drops.
tests/test_suture.py: the shell test now checks the groove reaches its depth in the BUILT skin, and that a head with
no detail hands the builder None.


# 27 Sep 2026 (later still): the spherical eye and the curved stalk

## anatomy/head.py
* sphere_eye(): a spherical eye (icosphere, subdivisions 4). Sessile: sunk into the cheek, centre 0.45 R above the
  base, so its underside is inside the shell. Stalked: stalk_arc() gives a centreline that leaves the root vertically
  and bends OUTWARD by eyeStalkBend along its length (an arc of the stalk's length); 14 frustums tapering
  0.45 R -> 0.32 R with spheres at the joints; the sphere sits on the end, 0.75 R along the tangent. Lenses are
  spherical caps on a lat-long lattice within +-arc/2 of outward and +-42 deg of the equator, same lens radius rule as
  the drum; below 0.5 mm they are omitted (holochroal: smooth). eyeLean still tips the whole thing forward about
  the root.
* eye_solid(): a stalked eye is ALWAYS the sphere on the curved stalk now (a drum on a stick was never right);
  sphere=True asks for a sphere on a sessile eye. The sessile drum is unchanged and still the default, so the frozen
  phacopid head (eyeSolid=1, no stalk) is bit-identical. No preset or reference uses a stalk.
* ornaments(): a spherical or stalked eye is not clipped at the cheek edge (it may overhang, as Asaphus kowalewskii's do).

## schema.py: eyeStalkBend [0, 80] deg, default 30; eyeSphere 0/1, default 0. Both in a1's "more". 103 parameters.

## tests/test_sphere_eye.py (head module only, 7): sessile default is still the drum; the sphere is a sphere (volume,
centre, sunk base); the stalked eye is one body carried outward by the bend, leaving the root vertically, the arc
the stalk's length and ending at the bend angle; bend 0 is straight; lenses only on the sphere within the arc; lean
tips it forward; HEAD.ornaments carries it standing above the cheek with its root in the shell.

## owed
blueprint.py's OUTWARD / BAND UNROLLED eye panels draw the DRUM lattice (lens_centres); for a sphere eye they need the
lat-long lattice. eyes/eyes_only.py likewise.


# 27 Sep 2026 (last): pelagic eyes, and the CHARACTERS table

## pelagic eyes are a setting of the sphere eye, not a new shape
Gon p. 44 (Carolinites, Opipeuter, the cyclopygids): eyes so large they are most of the head's sides, holochroal,
visual surface wrapping round. Two things stopped the sphere eye doing this:
* it sank 0.55 R into the cheek, which at R = 13 mm is through the floor. sphere_eye: sink = min(0.55 R, 3 mm + 0.15 R):
  identical below R = 7.5 mm, proud of the cheek above it. (test: the small sphere is unchanged)
* it could swallow the glabella. eye_geometry: xe = max(eyeLat wh, glabella edge + 0.85 R): an eye may overlap the
  glabella by 15 % of its radius at most. Checked against every preset's eye geometry before and after: none moved.
eyeSize's range: 0.45 -> 0.55 of the head half-width.

## schema.CHARACTERS, characters(P), express(P, *keys)
The guide's characters written down once: each with the settings that express it (`set`) and the test that reads it
off any parameter table (`has`), plus the guide page. 15 entries: holochroal / schizochroal / lost / pelagic /
stalked eyes, eye ridges, the three suture types, genal / pleural / pygidial spines, isopygous, effaced, olenimorph.
Only modelled characters are here. These thresholds are the decisions behind the tree page's LOCATE (which should now
read them from here instead of its own FEAT table) and behind /tree?from=<hash>.
tests/test_characters.py: express then read back for every key; exclusive suture types; exclusive eye types; every
preset reads.

## found while reading the presets
characters() reports schizochroalEyes for nine of the ten order presets. Their lensD / lensGap are the table defaults
(0.16 / 0.3), which are the phacopid values. Only phacopida should read schizochroal; the others want lensD ~0.05
(holochroal) or agnostida's eyesLost. A preset fix, not a code one, but LOCATE will be wrong until it is made.

## pelagic eyes, second pass (after the Carolinites / Pricyclopyge reconstructions): a bean, not a ball
* sphere_eye takes axial / tall (an ellipsoid: fore-aft and vertical stretch of the sphere, 1 = round) and sink_frac
  (0.55 = half sunk, the small-eye default; 0.88 = centre at cheek level so the eye bulges out past the margin AND under
  it). Lenses go on the ellipsoid, pushed in along its normal. The blend mound under a solid eye is elliptical too
  (exact no-op at eyeAxial = 1).
* eyeCollar: the cheek rises around the eye's rim by this fraction of R (a fillet), so a sunk eye grows out of the
  head instead of sitting on it. 0 by default = the old cheek, bit for bit.
* schema: eyeAxial [0.6, 1.8], eyeTall [0.6, 1.5], eyeSink [0.2, 1.0] (default 0.55), eyeCollar [0, 0.6]. 107 params.
  CHARACTERS["pelagicEyes"].set: eyeSize 0.36, eyeArc 280, eyeLat 0.72, eyePos 0.52, eyeAxial 1.45, eyeTall 1.05,
  eyeSink 0.88, eyeCollar 0.35, holochroal.
* tests: the pelagic eye is longer fore-aft than wide, overhangs the margin, reaches at most 15 % into the glabella,
  extends both above and below the cheek, and unions with the head into one closed body; the collar is zero by
  default and full height on the rim.
* still short of the reference: the eyes should reach the front margin (eyePos ~0.6 with this bean); the bean is a
  true ellipsoid, not kidney-shaped on its inner side.

## pelagic eyes, third pass (checked against the Carolinites reconstruction, dorsal + lateral): a HANGING eye
What was wrong with the second pass, panel by panel: the eye sat on the cheek where Carolinites' hangs under it (top
at the dorsal surface, globe reaching below the thorax); it sat mid-head where the real ones are the front corners;
the head was broad where the real cephalon is no wider than the thorax, the eyes being the width; the lens band was
symmetric about the equator where the real visual surface faces out and DOWN; and it was a fore-aft ellipsoid where
the real eye is taller than wide.
* eyeSink range to 2.2: 1.85 puts the centre 0.85 R below the cheek, the globe's top at the palpebral shelf.
* the lens band: for a hanging eye (sink_frac > 1, sessile) lenses run from 22 deg above the equator to 78 deg
  below (HANG_LENS_ELEV); otherwise +-42 as before.
* CHARACTERS["pelagicEyes"].set: eyeSize 0.40, eyeArc 300, eyePos 0.78 (front corners), eyeLat 0.66, eyeAxial 1.05,
  eyeTall 1.25, eyeSink 1.85, eyeCollar 0.10, widthHeadFront 0.70 (a narrow head), glabInflate 1.5, holochroal.
* test: taller than wide; its front reaches the front of the head; overhangs the margin; top within 0.3 R of the
  shelf, bottom more than 1.5 R below it; one closed body with the head.
* next: the cheek should wrap DOWN over the globe's upper outer side (a hood), as the reconstruction shows, instead of
  the globe emerging from the shell's flat underside. A negative eyeCollar (a socket dip around the eye) with a
  smaller sink would do it, but the dip must respect the shell's floor.

## pelagic eyes, fourth pass: the head wraps round the eye (a helmet), not a plate with globes under it
The cephalon in the Carolinites reconstruction is strongly vaulted: its dorsal surface curves down at the sides and
the eye is set into that flank, top at the apex. The dome mode (tent 1) is centred at -0.19 Lc and has run out by
the front corners, so the flank there was flat at margin height. The v4 vault with a long parallel section keeps the
head full-height forward: CHARACTERS["pelagicEyes"].set now carries tent 0, fulcrum 0.5, cephParallel 0.42,
marginHeight 0.06, headRelief 1.35, borderWidth 0, widthHeadFront 0.74, glabInflate 1.4; the eye at eyePos 0.60,
eyeLat 0.80 (in the flank), eyeSink 1.0 (centre at the flank's surface), eyeTall 1.15, eyeAxial 1.10, no collar.
Result on the test head: flank at the eye 0.39 of the apex, globe top at the apex, bottom below the margin.
NB tent / fulcrum / marginHeight are body-wide: expressing the character vaults the thorax as well (Carolinites'
thorax is vaulted too, but it is a consequence to know about). The character's test reads eyeSize + eyeArc only.

## pelagic eyes, fifth pass: vaultRound (anatomy/common.py)
"Even more curve" ran out of parameters: vault()'s outer flank is 0.65 (1 - s^1.6), which cannot get steeper. New
Form parameter vaultRound m in [0.3, 1]: outer = 0.65 (clip(1 - s^1.6, 0, 1))^m. 1.0 = the v4 profile, bit for bit
(tested against the legacy expression on 401 points; the branch is skipped entirely at 1.0); 0.62 = a quarter
circle; 0.45 = a full top with near-vertical sides. The clip matters: u runs past 1 at pleural tips and genal arms,
where the legacy form goes slightly negative and a fractional power would give NaN (it did, once).
vault() is shared with the thorax, so this is body-wide like tent / fulcrum / marginHeight; re-ran test_schema (the
three frozen readings), test_regression and test_mesh: all unchanged. CHARACTERS["pelagicEyes"].set: vaultRound 0.45.
Flank at the eye 0.44 of the apex, the head wrapping round the globe.

## pelagic eyes, sixth pass: matched to the reconstruction's lateral, then nudged
Side elevation against the Carolinites lateral: the proportions were right (eye ~0.5 head length, ~head height) but
the globe poked above the crest and ahead of the front. Placement, not size: eyeSink 1.45 tucked it under the crest,
eyePos 0.62 put its front at the head's front. Then, on request, inboard and up: eyeLat 0.84 -> 0.74, eyeSink 1.45
-> 1.25. Flank at the eye 0.51 of the apex; globe top 0.94 of the apex, bottom 0.35 apex below the bed.
