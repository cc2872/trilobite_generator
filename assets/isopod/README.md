## What it is (measured 27 Sep 2026)
The main body only; the legs are separate parts that attach to it (not in the repo yet).

- 8 print-in-place pieces: head, 6 segments, tail. Overall 79 × 178 × 37 mm, 143 cm³. Mirror-symmetric about the
  midline to 0.004 mm.
- Every piece stands on the bed (one flat plane, z = 7.33 in the file's frame), so nothing needs supports under
  the joints.
- Joints: a ball, radius 5.75 mm, centred on the midline 7.5 mm above the bed, on a neck that rises from the bed at
  the front of each piece. It sits in a spherical socket in the piece ahead, open at the bottom. A 1 mm slit splits
  every ball and neck down the midline. Ball joints, so the chain wiggles on more than one axis.
- Clearance 0.244 mm wherever neighbouring pieces face each other closely: ball in socket, and between the columns.
- Each piece in side view is a column standing on the bed with a dorsal plate on top. The front lip of each plate
  tucks under the plate of the piece ahead, like overlapping tergites, so the joints are hidden from above.
- The plates overlap but never touch: measured 20 mm off the midline, each overlap is 7-8 mm long with a 1.4-5 mm
  air gap between the upper plate and the lip under it. The upper plate's underside slopes up toward the rear, so
  the gap is a wedge. That wedge is the room the lip swings through when the chain bends. The upper plates are
  2.6-4 mm thick, thinning to a feather edge at the rim.
- Leg sockets: two bowls in the underside of the head and each of the first five segments at ±23 mm from the
  midline (±16 mm on the tail), radius ~5.5 mm, open to the bed, about 7.8 mm deep.
- About 8 % of the surface overhangs by more than 45°; the downward-facing plate edges are short.
