# CHANGES 24 Oct 2026 — the genal spine bug, the studio horn, smoother curved spines

Delivered as a patch against the 23 Oct 2026 tree. The photo log of the run is in the project doc `claude/overnight-2026-10-24.md`.

## What was wrong (from the code, 00:17)

1. **The stub at the genal corner** = the notch wall. `shaped_head.py` lowered the rear margin inboard of the spine's inner
   edge by `notch × spine length` and held the columns between that edge and the genal angle at `yg` — a step of
   `notch·Lh`. Hidden under a band that runs straight back (the crescent kit), exposed the moment the band leaves at an angle.
2. **Spines through the pygidium** (shot 1) = the column representation. The silhouette is one front and one rear edge per
   x-column and the band is unioned in by per-column max; a band turning inward puts band and head in the same columns, the
   head's rear margin becomes the band's upper edge and the solid fills down to it.
3. **Tip half a head-width behind the cursor** = `HEADDY/1` in the studio's spine drag; `HEADDY` is model mm, needed `/ sc`.
4. Canvas fit ignored the spine's reach (long spine leaves the canvas).

## What this does not do (deliberately)

- The flat band is still the silhouette's column envelope: it runs outward only. A spine that curves in, hooks, or rises is the
  genal **horn** (a solid of its own, now editable in the studio). A curved *blade* (Lobopyge's broad pleura-continuation) is
  neither yet — that needs the spine as a swept height field, a representation change left for later.
- Pleural spines, fringe spines, the pygidium: untouched (different code).
- `genalHornYaw` defaults to the old fixed 10°, `flare` only on the genal horn: every existing preset builds the same geometry
  apart from the smoother arc facets.
