# Changes, 20 October 2026

## TriloMorph: the landmark protocol, and the parameter box set from real cephala (schema 6.3)

TriloMorph (Serra, Balseiro et al. 2023, *Sci Data* 10:841; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.8033445; CC BY 4.0) is a 2-D landmark
database of trilobite cephala and pygidia in dorsal view, one adult specimen per genus. The snapshot read today holds
485 specimens / 381 genera (Devonian-heavy). It carries no thorax and no enrolment: it enters the generator's **anatomy
and parameter box only**. `instrument.py`, `joints/pin.py`, `tests/references` and PREREG § 3 are untouched.

### landmarks.py: their protocol, read off our plans
The twelve formula-points of 11 Sep are replaced (kept in `legacy/landmarks_v1.py`). `cephalon(P)` and `pygidium(P)`
return Serra et al.'s 16 + 7 landmarks and 4 + 3 semilandmark curves (glabella LM2–LM7, facial suture LM10–LM12, anterior
margin LM13–LM15, posterior margin LM4–LM15; pygidial axis, border furrow, margin), taken from `anatomy.head.plan` and
`anatomy.tail.plan` — the functions the meshes are sampled from — in their frame (right half, anterior +y, mm). What is
placed rather than measured is listed in the module docstring (LM2 from `preglabField`; the occipital furrow at 0.13 Lc;
the suture from `sutureEnd`; LM15 as the genal angle, their Fig. 3 alternative; blind forms collapse LM8/9/14 onto LM10
as their files do). `tps_string(specimens, part)` writes one block per animal with `CURVES=`/`POINTS=` in their default
template (12/20/20/20), `IMAGE=<id>_C.png`, `SCALE=1.0`; `shapRead()` and `geomorph::readland.tps` both read it.
`/api/tps` takes `part=C|P`; the generator page's button downloads both files. Checked by overlaying the landmarks on
built pin-model heads (proetida, phacopida, harpetida): the margins, the genal tip and the eye ring coincide with the mesh.

### The generator in TriloMorph's morphospace (the reason for the parameter changes)
GPA + PCA on the 16 fixed cephalic landmarks: 286 sighted, complete TriloMorph cephala plus the ten order presets and the
default animal (classic head). PC1 39 %, PC2 19 %, PC3 10 % of the real variance.
* The presets lie inside the real cloud's hull but on its sparse right side; 6 of 10 are within the real specimens'
  own 95th-percentile nearest-neighbour distance (0.205), 4 are not (harpetida 0.227, lichida 0.243, phacopida 0.248,
  the default 0.221). Nearest real genera: asaphida→Priobole, corynexochida→Cummingella, odontopleurida→Porterfieldia,
  proetida→Semiproetus, ptychopariida→Chungkingaspis, redlichiida→Paraglobusia, phacopida→Denckmannites, harpetida→Tawstockia.
* The dense real cluster (Boeckops and the Devonian phacopids: wide heads, large eyes set far back and far out) is reached
  by no preset. The phacopida preset sits at its edge.
* Mean-shape offsets of the generator vs the real mean, largest first: the eye's rear end (LM9) 0.10 Procrustes units too
  far forward and the eye too medial (LM14); the posterior sagittal points (LM3, LM4) too lateral — i.e. our heads are too
  narrow for their length (half-width / length 0.60–0.72 vs the real median 0.90); the suture end (LM12) and genal angle
  (LM15) too medial. Figures: `morphospace_trilomorph_vs_generator.png`, `mean_shapes.png` (in the review bundle).
These are preset-level findings (eyePos, eyeSize, eyeLat, widthHeadFront per order) and are **not** changed here; they
want a decision with the Harvard side and the fit of real specimens (`fit.py`).

### schema.py 6.3
* `preglabField` (new, Head; 0.02–0.35, default 0.10): the flat field between the glabella's front and the anterior
  margin / head length. Wired in `anatomy/head.py` as `dpf = 0.10 - preglabField`: the glabellar relief fade
  (0.80–0.92 Lc) and the axial-furrow end (0.90 Lc) move by `dpf`. **At the default the head height-field is bit-identical
  to 6.2** (checked on five presets over a 241×241 grid); `dpf` is exactly 0.0, not a float residue. TriloMorph: median 0.06,
  5–95 % 0.00–0.30.
* `axisFrac` range 0.20–0.50 → 0.20–**0.70** (TriloMorph glabella half-width / cephalic half-width: median 0.41, 95th pct 0.66;
  the old box clipped the inflated-glabella third of the data).
* `genalSpine` range 0–1.5 → 0–**2.5** (95th pct 1.40, max 3.4).
* `eyeLat` default 0.0 → **0.5** (TriloMorph eye centre ≈ 0.5 of the half-width; the "hug the glabella" rule was the
  outlier). Every order preset sets `eyeLat` explicitly, so no preset moves; the table default (and `python
  schema.py` / an animal that omits the key) does.
* Adding a key changes every `param_hash` (the hash is md5 of the full coerced table), so every cache key and the
  frozen references' recorded hashes change, as they did at 6.1 and 6.2 (13 Oct review, item 24). Readings do not.
* Widening a range widens the *buildable* morphospace of H2. Do this before Block 2 is locked, and write the box as
  "TriloMorph 5th–95th percentile, release <X>" in `PREREG_block2_percolation.md`.

### Noted, not changed
The pin-model head mesh is not bit-reproducible run to run (same P, same code: proetida 17649 vs 17660 vertices, different
hashes), from Manifold's threaded booleans. Readings are what the references freeze, and they are compared with tolerances,
so nothing here depends on it, but a mesh-hash regression test would be meaningless; the plan's height-field is deterministic.

## The studio's default head: why it drew a bell with two sticks, and the fix

**Why.** `shaped_head.OUTLINE` (the base every head's outline edits sit on) was the crescent *print piece's* own
silhouette (head3d: a round dome widest right at the rear, `front_exp` 2.0 / `widest` 0.08; two parallel round-ended
bands a sixth of the half-width wide, `genal_w` 0.167 / `genal_tip` 1.0; and a U notch as deep as the bands are long,
`notch` 0.9). That U is the kit's rear margin, so the thorax rows were drawn *inside* the head. Every named style had
already overridden those keys (`SP = genal_w 0.10, genal_tip 0.12, notch 0.08, widest 0.03`); only the default — the
"plain trilobite" the studio opens on, and any head the editor started from "crescent" — kept the kit's shape. The
mechanics do not need it: the shaped heads build without the notch and the rows model clears the head sweep.

**Fix.**
* `OUTLINE` is now an anatomical head: `front_exp` 1.6 (ogival), `widest` 0.03, `genal` 0.6, `genal_w` 0.10,
  `genal_tip` 0.12, `genal_spread` 4, `notch` 0.08. The kit's silhouette is kept as `shaped_head.CRESCENT_OUTLINE`
  and as the outline of the name "crescent" (`head_styles.OUTLINES["crescent"]`), so a head saved on "crescent"
  resolves to the same numbers as before.
* Every named style now states the eight keys the old defaults supplied (`head_styles._OLD`, filled with
  `setdefault`), so no named silhouette moved: checked style by style against the shipped module (max |Δ| = 0).
* New style **"plain"** (`head_styles.STYLES["plain"]`, outline `{}` = the new base, the crescent's face). The studio
  and the head editor open on it; `/api/head_styles` lists plain, crescent, then the guide. "crescent" still means
  the isopod head piece when built (`_head_style` → None → `IBODY.build(P, "isopod")`), as before. The plain head
  builds: 11 pieces, 114 s, `print_valid` true.
* **Pointiness** is `front_exp` (1.2 sharp, 1.6 ogival, 2 round, 4 blunt); the front handle already drags it sideways,
  and both editors now label the slider so. The default is pointed.
* `OUTLINE_RANGE["genal"]` 2.5 → 3.5: the **cryptolithus** style (genal 2.6) could not be drawn or built in the shipped
  code (`ValueError` from `outline_of`); it draws now. `tests/test_plain_and_trace.py::test_every_style_draws` keeps it so.

## Trace a photo: the head's margin from clicked points (head editor)

A TriloMorph-style way to make a head: in `/head`, choose a dorsal photo or drawing (it stays in the browser; nothing is
uploaded), turn tracing on, click the FRONT of the head on the axis and the REAR margin on the axis, then click along the
RIGHT margin from the front back to the genal angle, and "use trace". The page turns the clicks into the margin in
half-width units (axis from the two points, half-width from the widest click), sends it as `outline.trace`, and from
then on draws the photo through that frame — rotated and scaled so the traced axis lies on the plan's axis — with the
plan on top, so photo and drawing can be compared directly. The spine stays parametric (`genal`, `genal_w`, `genal_tip`,
`genal_spread`, `genal_curve`), attached at the traced genal angle; the glabella, eye, brim and crown handles still work;
the front / widest / angle / notch handles are dropped while a trace is in force (they would fight it). "undo", "clear",
an opacity slider; × on the trace in Edits drops it. Saved heads keep the trace (`web/heads/<name>.json`, key
`outline.trace`), so a traced head is usable from the studio's picker and in `/api/build` like any saved head.

Implementation: `shaped_head.trace_of` validates (3–400 finite points, |x| ≤ 1.5, −4 ≤ y ≤ 1, folded to the right half,
first point snapped to the axis and required to be the front); `Shape` takes the traced polygon's per-column extent as
the body's front and rear edges (smoothed σ = 3 columns against the hand's jitter), reads length, widest line and genal
angle off it, and runs the same spine, margin and surface code as a parametric outline. `diagram()` returns the trace
and drops the four margin handles. `/api/head_diagram`, `/api/heads` and `/api/build` take it inside `outline` (or at
the top level of the head spec). Known limits: a spine traced as part of the margin is filled column-wise (a strongly
inward-curling traced spine would web); trace the body and leave the spine to its keys. Checked headlessly (Chromium):
load photo → 7 clicks → use trace → the plan follows the clicks, handles reduce to spine/root/eye/glabella/crown → save
→ reload with the trace → the studio opens on "plain".

## TriloMorph specimens as saved heads (`scripts/trilomorph_import.py`)

TriloMorph's own code (`TriloMorph-funs.R`) does not trace photographs: their landmarks are placed by hand in tpsDig or
StereoMorph, and the R functions read, validate and resample them. What can be reused is their *data*: each cephalon's
anterior and posterior margin curves are a hand-traced margin in exactly the form the trace tool takes. The importer reads
a TriloMorph checkout (`.txt` StereoMorph and `.tps` files) and writes one saved head per specimen into `web/heads/`:
the margin LM1 → anterior curve → LM15 → posterior curve → LM4 as `outline.trace` (72 points, half-width units, axis from
LM1–LM4), the genal spine (length |LM16−LM15| / sagittal length, spread from its angle) as the spine keys, and the eye
(radius = ½ LM8–LM9, position, lateral from LM14) and glabella (axis_frac from LM6, glab_inflate = LM5/LM6) as face keys.
`doc` and a `source` block carry the specimen ID, genus, the paper, the Zenodo DOI and CC BY 4.0, so the credit travels
with the head. Nothing of their images is read.

    python scripts/trilomorph_import.py <checkout> --genus Phacops Harpes Deiphon      # or --id <ID> ..., or --all

328 of their 377 cephala import (49 lack a margin curve or a landmark; 25 files have no metadata). Ten were imported
here and drawn (`trilomorph_heads_imported.png`): Bumastus, Deiphon, Eldredgeops, Encrinurus, Harpes, Odontopleura,
Phacops (×2), Triarthrus, Walliserops — the first heads in the generator whose silhouettes are a real specimen's. Two of
them were built through `/api/build` (see below). Known limits: a harpetid's posterior curve runs round the inside of
the prolongation, so Harpes imports as its outer margin only; marginal spines (Odontopleura) and an anterior trident
(Walliserops) come through the column rule as bumps and prongs rather than as spines; `width` (head vs thorax) is not in
their data and keeps 1.15. Tests: `tests/test_trilomorph_import.py` (a synthetic head in a rotated photo frame round-trips:
axis, spine length and spread, eye and glabella keys; blind and spineless cases; both file readers).

### Their landmarking tool, kept; its file read by the editor (`/api/heads/import`)
TriloMorph's points are placed in tpsDig2 (a closed Windows program) or StereoMorph's `digitizeImages()` (R, GPL): neither
can be embedded in the site, and neither needs to be. The head editor now takes the file they produce: "or a .tps / .txt"
beside the trace controls → `POST /api/heads/import` (multipart `file` or the raw text) → the same reading as the importer
(`scripts/trilomorph_import.head_from`) → the editor loads the margin as a trace, the spine, eye and glabella keys as edits,
names the head after the file and records "landmarked in tpsDig / StereoMorph with the TriloMorph protocol" in `doc`. Nothing
is stored until the user saves. Checked headlessly with a real TriloMorph `.tps` (AMNH_28896, Eldredgeops): 16 landmarks,
four curves, the head drawn, saved and reloaded; `test_trilomorph_import.py::test_import_endpoint_reads_a_tps_upload`.
