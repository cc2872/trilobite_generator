# Changes, 5–6 Sep 2026  (schema 4.1)

Mechanism untouched: pin hinge, stop bevel, clearance, barrel, knuckles. Everything below is form.

## Form fitted from the reference sculpt (35.3 × 66.1 × 8.7 mm STL)
- `vault()` gains the **tent** profile: Gaussian axial dome + straight pleural slope (5 params, 0.12 mm rms on seg2). Knobs `tent`, `axisSigma`, `pleuralSlope`.
- Segments gain convexity along the body: `ringArch`, `bladeCamber`. Humps vanish at both hinge lines and under the flap.
- Head: superellipse outline (`headOutlineExp`, ref 2.15) and superellipsoid dome (`headDomeExp` 1.5, `headDomeFill` 0.82). Occipital ring and rear band guards keep the hinge and the shingle over seg0 valid whatever the dome does.
- `bladeChord`: rib fore-aft width beyond the root / pitch. 1.3 = v4 tiled plates; 0.9 = separate ribs (reference).

## Spines are arcs, not cones
- Pleural spines: continuation of the rib's plan curve (`edges()`), chord closing to a point, drooping in z.
- Genal spines: horns of a crescent (`head_edges()`), riding at pleural-margin height.
- Tail: plan parametrised by angle round the margin; forks and marginal spines are radial continuations. No cones on the tail. Grid 53 columns when it has points (73 stalls the fit; needle roots don't tessellate).
- Bevel band collapses to axis + rib root when `bladeChord < 1` (with in-plane spines the wide band would slice them off).
- `print_validity`: cone "spine base" rule removed.
- Still cones: axial spine, occipital spine.

## Presets and the sculpt dial
- `schema.PRESETS`: `reference` (effaced) and `textured` (ours, default). Both built from `_FORM` + `_sculpt(level)`.
- Macro `sculpt` (0 → 1, default 1) drives furrows, effacement, glabella, eyes, border, tail rings. Slide it off if you want it off.
- `presets/*.json`: species built 6 Sep (olenellid, paradoxidid, phacopid, asaphid, calymenid, agnostid, spiny, elongate, macropygous). Load with `schema.coerce({**schema.defaults(), **json.load(f)['params']})`.

## Robustness
- `prune_slivers()` drops zero-volume slivers and fragments < 2 % of the main solid; records them in `trilobite.BUILD_NOTES`. Never silent.
- Hinge web reaches `ring_top + 1` (it used to touch the shell by 0.1 mm; the dome head detached its whole hinge block).
- `instrument.overlap_volume`: when a mesh isn't a closed volume, a chunked Monte-Carlo estimate (6 000 samples, ±0.3 mm³) replaces the binary FCL guess. Slow (~50 s per pass); the real fix is the seg stop-block seam that tessellates non-watertight (2 non-manifold edges at x = ±axisFrac·w).

## Known
- A failed OCC fuse returns the tool, not an error (this is how the tail "vanished" and how paradoxidid seg9 produced a −178 000 mm³ body). Add a volume-vs-plan-area sanity check to `print_validity`.
- Species gallery poses at e = 1.0 are unmeasured; only textured, reference, spiny, elongate, macropygous, phacopid(v1) have instrument numbers.
- Ribs are 2 mm shells, not solid rolled ribs like the reference.

## Head / tail shell knobs (schema 4.3)
`headWall`, `tailWall` (thickness / wall), `headRelief`, `tailRelief` (apex height / relief), `tailDomeExp` (along-body fall-off; 2 = v4).
Defaults reproduce 4.2 exactly. Guards: the occipital ring and the tail axis always hold ring_top over their hinges; segment 0's
stepped front follows headWall. Stress test (1.6 / 1.6 / 1.25 / 0.75 / 3.2): builds sane, no rest contact, 10 mm³ seg5–tail overlap
at the stop — a 3.2 mm tail shell hangs below what the ½·maxAngle bevel was cut for. The ruler reports it; it does not hide it.

## Head plan arc (schema 4.4)
`headRearArc` (rear edge bows back from the axis to the genal angles, fraction of Lc; 0 = v4) and `headRearExp` (1 = thin crescent
band, high = thick band that bows late). Horns now start from the concave rear edge; the crescent's arms behind the hinge line are
a hood held above the ribs (`z ≥ thoracic vault + blade camber + wall + clearance`). Built at arc 0.4 / horns 0.5: sane, no head
contact at rest or at the stop. See renders/head_arc_params.png.

**OPEN — measured regression on the default.** Rebuilding `textured` under 4.4 gave seg5–tail 1.0 mm³ at rest and 17.2 mm³ at the
stop (4.2 measured none), and the tail needed a grid-jitter rebuild. It is not the head arc (identical numbers with and without it).
Suspects, in order: the 4.3 tail hinge guard (now only active when tailRelief < 1, untested after that change), the 4.3 tail tz
rewrite, `_build_checked`'s jittered tail. Bisect by building the textured tail alone under 4.2 vs 4.4 and comparing solids/volume.
Until then the 4.2 default numbers are the trusted ones.

## Crescent arm thickness (schema 4.5)
`genalWidth` is now the arm's thickness ACROSS (plan width / wh; default 0.12, harpetid 0.30), not a chord — the old definition
made a 2 mm sliver whatever you set. `genalTaper` shapes the arm's end (0.5 blunt … 3 pointed). Arms are the head's lateral band
run backward; inboard of wh they hood the ribs (thoracic vault + camber + wall + clearance), outboard they ride at margin height.
Textured default horns change shape slightly (were a sliver; now a 4 mm pointed arm). Known cosmetic: a small spike at the arm's
outer tip where yr = yf + 1.4 closes the column. See renders/head_arm_thickness.png.

## Genal arm from a formula (schema 4.6)
- `genalPath` (kind "expr"): the arm centreline's sideways offset as a formula in s ∈ [0,1] (root → tip), numpy math only
  (sin cos tan exp log sqrt abs tanh arctan pi e). Normalised so `genalCurve` sets the amount; e.g. 's', 's**2', 'sin(pi*s/2)',
  '-0.6*s+1.6*s**2'. Bad input falls back to 's**2' with a BUILD_NOTES entry. `safe_expr()` in trilobite.py.
- `genalWidthMM`: absolute arm thickness in mm (0 = use genalWidth as a fraction of half-width). `genalTaper` shapes the end.
- The arm is a band of that width along the path; columns are cut from the path numerically (`arm_band`). z: hood over ribs
  inboard of the head's side, margin height outboard. Schema/coerce accept string params; the site shows a text box for them
  (KNOBS now carry kind and help; PRESETS on the site include schema.PRESETS).
- Built '-0.6*s+1.6*s**2', 9 mm, 25°, 0.8 Lc: see renders/formula_arms_built.png.

## Arms as swept bands; inward paths (schema 4.6, evening)
The x-column head outline cannot hold an arm that turns inward (it filled the crescent's inside with web). The arms are now
separate swept bands along the genalPath centreline (width W_s, end taper), starting 12 % inside the head and following the
head's own surface there so the fuse is solid; outside they hood the ribs inboard / ride at margin height outboard. genalCurve
may be negative to mirror any path. Formulas that go negative turn inward: '-s', '-sin(pi*s)', 's-1.6*s**2' (claw).
head_edges() is body-only again. renders/head_arms_swept.png. Not yet measured against the thorax at the stop.

## New default (schema 4.7)
`textured` re-proportioned: 150 mm, 9 segments (pitch 8.5), cephFrac 0.30 / pygFrac 0.22, forks 0.35, genal spines 0.32 Lc on
's**2' at 12°, 5 mm, headRearArc 0.12, eyes 0.16 @ 0.62. `reference` untouched (still the 6-segment STL fit). `_FORM` untouched.
`_build_checked` now also retries when a negative-volume solid was pruned during the build (seg3/seg4 of this default failed
their first boolean and were rebuilt with grid jitter ±2). renders/default_v4_7.png. Not yet instrument-measured.
Known false positive: `_sane`'s footprint floor uses the bounding box, so segments with very long in-plane spines (olenellid
seg2, paradoxidid seg12) are reported UNSANE though they built; use the rib's plan area instead.
