# Prompt 2 — mesh.py (OpenCascade-free geometry core)
- mesh.py: symmetric height-field shells and envelopes (sampled on the right half, mirrored; pinched rows collapse to one
  vertex), Manifold union/difference/intersection, box/cylinder/frustum/wedge primitives, mirror_x, surface-based
  is_symmetric, and hinge() — a parameter-for-parameter port of trilobite.add_hinge. Shell builds in ~0.1 s, hinge in ~0.3 s.
- Blur is scale-invariant (passes scale with grid density against the 53-wide BREP reference grid), so the default
  121x61 grid adds resolution without changing shape.
- Finding: the BREP B-spline fit sits ~0.25 mm below the exact height field on a 12 mm dome (sides agree to 0.05 mm).
  The presets were tuned on that bias. Regression tolerance for surfaces stays 0.30 mm; the mesh builder is the faithful one.
- Finding: after a Manifold boolean the geometry stays mirror-symmetric but flat-face triangulation may not; symmetry is
  tested on the surface (sampled points), not the vertex set.
- Boolean results drop zero-volume sliver components (coincident-face artifacts). Post-boolean mirror asymmetry measured
  0.03 mm worst case; is_symmetric tolerance is 0.05 mm.
- tests/test_mesh.py: 12 tests, all passing here (build123d present, so the BREP comparison ran). Nothing in the existing pipeline uses mesh.py yet (prompt 3 wires the parts).
