# CHANGES 30 Oct 2026 — the Raup prism page (/prism)

Delivered as a patch against GitHub `main` 630405f (branch `feat/prism`).

## What it is

`/prism` draws the paper's Raup figure (panel b) as a live page: base = head / thorax / tail length fractions (a triangle,
they sum to 1), height = thoracic segments. The white diamond column is the generator's schema range. Dots: the made
library (coloured by rear width), TriloMorph whole-body fossils (white photos, grey drawings), the 25 Gon genera as built
(rings), the ten presets (squares).

- **Click any point and it is built.** Its parameters go to the generator the way the studio's View 3D sends them
  (sessionStorage `trilo_body_draft`, `/generator?...&bodydraft=1&only3d=1`), and the animal appears in the panel, laid
  flat. Measured (pin, seconds) or print (isopod model, a minute or more) by the toggle.
  - a **preset**: `/api/preset/<name>`;
  - a **made animal**: its preset + the eleven sweep values it was made with, so it is the library animal exactly;
  - a **fossil or genus**: its head, tail, segment count and rear width on its order's preset (Olenida → ptychopariida,
    Trinucleida → asaphida, unknown → the nearest preset). Values outside the schema are clipped and the page says which.
- **"You"**: the orange marker is the animal built, with a drop line to the floor; the four sliders (head, tail, segments,
  rear width) move it, and releasing a slider rebuilds. A white ring stays where the pick started. Under the sliders: the
  head / thorax / tail split, the nearest fossil, and how many made animals sit close by.
- **From the other pages:** a `prism` button in the studio (sends the studio's draft: `?mine=1`, head and body projected
  onto the parameters by `/api/macro`, built on the isopod model so it is the studio's own animal) and in the generator
  (`?p=` with the share link's encoding).

- a real mouse click on a made animal → built in 15 s (pin), shown flat;
- a fossil (*Basidechenella*) → proetida with its proportions, built in 11 s;

- studio `prism` button → "my trilobite" from the studio's draft.

## Known limits

- The fossils are proportions only; the rest of each fossil's animal is its order's preset.
- The genera use their recipe's four values on their preset, not the full recipe (head style, outline, spines live in
  `build_genera.py`, which is not on GitHub).
- A studio body edited row by row is placed by `/api/macro`'s projection, an approximation (the build itself is exact).
