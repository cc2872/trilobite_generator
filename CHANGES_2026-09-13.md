plan shape (ky, kidney), lunate taper, lens styles raised / sunken (socket + sclera rim + convex

stalk with outward/forward lean, min_D threshold as a parameter.
Not wired into cephalon(); call directly.

renders dorsal / oblique / lateral / close-up. Per-type overrides for eyeSize, eyePos, eyeLat.
Backface + frame culling in the renderer (the 460k-face tower killed matplotlib without it).

## manifold3d pin (2026-09-15, Block 2 start)
- Root-caused test_prongs_build_on_head_and_tail (head prongs broke L/R symmetry): NOT our code.
  manifold3d 3.5.3's Manifold.cylinder corrupts the seam vertex of each ring, (r,0)->(r,r), so
  every M.frustum spine/prong carried a spurious sqrt(2)*r spike (~0.4 mm), failing is_symmetric.
- Fixed by pinning manifold3d==3.0.1 (verified clean: 0 bad verts, prong test green). Satisfies
  PREREG §9's "requirements.txt with pinned versions". agnostida/asaphida readings are byte-identical
  on 3.0.1 vs 3.5.3 (the spike never touched their measurements), so no reference re-freeze needed.
