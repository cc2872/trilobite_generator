# CHANGES 1 Nov 2026 — the genal piece, rebuilt as parts you assemble (schema 6.7)

Branch `feat/genal-shapes` off GitHub `main` 630405f. Replaces the genal-spine controls in both heads: the generator's
(`anatomy/head.py`) and the studio's (`isopod_model/shaped_head.py`).

## Why

The old spine could only be a band that left the genal angle and ran outward: the studio drew it into the head's
columns (one front and one rear edge per x), so a spine that turned forward, in over the body, or back on itself was
cut ("use the genal horn"); the generator's arm drifted sideways along a formula (`genalPath`, `genalCurve`). Many
real heads were out of reach.

## The new piece (`anatomy/genal.py`, one module for both heads)

1. **Direction, the full circle.** A capsule from the root on the margin, aimed anywhere: 0 straight back, +90 out,
   180 forward, −90 in over the body.
2. **A kink.** From a point along it (a fraction of the length), a second capsule aimed anywhere on its own. The two
   meet in a round joint.
3. **Pointed to round.** The tip runs from a point (0: the width tapers to nothing) to round (1: a parallel band with a
   round end). Short and round makes a lobe or a rounded genal angle; long and pointed a spine.
4. **A web.** An edge from an anchor on the rear margin to the spine's tip, filling the corner. Its roundness:
   0 a straight edge, +1 a curve that hugs the corner (a fillet), −1 a curve that bulges out.

Each part is a plan band built as a height field (`Piece.bands`); the pieces are unioned. The root is where the spine
left before (the genal angle; or moved along the margin by the studio's `genal_at`).

## Keys

| generator (schema 6.7) | studio outline | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `genalSpine` (kept) | `genal` (kept) | length / head length |
| `genalWidth`, `genalWidthMM` (kept) | `genal_w` (kept) | width at the root |
| `genalAngle` (new) | `genal_angle` (new) | direction, deg, −180..180 |
| `genalKink` (new) | `genal_kink` (new) | where it bends, / length (0 = straight) |
| `genalKinkAngle` (new) | `genal_kink_angle` (new) | direction after the kink |
| `genalTip` (new) | `genal_tip` (same meaning, now 0..1) | 0 pointed .. 1 round |
| `genalWeb` (new) | `genal_web` (new) | web anchor: 0 none .. 1 at the axial furrow |
| `genalWebRound` (new) | `genal_web_round` (new) | 0 straight, +1 hugging, −1 bulging |
| `genalCurve`, `genalPath`, `genalTaper` (gone) | `genal_spread`, `genal_curve` (gone) | |

`genalSweep`, `headRearArc`, the genal horn (`genalHorn*`) and `genal_at` are unchanged.

## Old animals still load

- `schema.coerce()` converts old keys (`schema.migrate_genal`): the old centreline (straight back, drifting sideways by
  tan(genalCurve) × length × genalPath) becomes two straight pieces through its root, middle and tip (kink at 0.5; one
  piece when nearly straight), and `genalTaper` (0.5 blunt .. 3 point) becomes `genalTip`. The library CSVs, saved
  animals and old presets all load. `schema.migrate()` reports the conversion.
- The ten presets and `presets_other/pillbug.json` are rewritten with the new keys (same values as the conversion).
  Landmark 16 (the spine tip) moves by at most 1.1 mm on any preset (redlichiida: 38.3, −33.1 → 38.0, −32.0).
- Studio heads saved before today: `shaped_head.migrate_outline` converts `genal_spread`/`genal_curve` the same way.
  The head styles in `head_styles.py` are rewritten with the new keys.

## Controls

- **Studio** (`web/studio.html`): the tip grip turns the spine the whole way round its root (or round the kink, when
  there is one); a **kink** grip sits mid-spine (drag it to bend a straight spine); a **web** grip sits on the rear
  margin beside the root (drag it inward to grow a web). Each grip's panel has its sliders: tip — length, direction,
  width, pointed-to-round; kink — where, direction after; web — anchor, roundness.
- **Generator** (`web/index.html`): the sketch's genal grip is polar about the root (length, direction; with a kink,
  the part after it); the cheek panel has sliders for the six new keys.
- `web/head.html` (the older head editor): ranges and the tip grip updated.

## Other edits

- `anatomy/head.py`: the arm is built from `genal.Piece`; `genal_solid(S)` unions its bands (also used by
  `joints/flexi.py`). Inside the head's outline a piece runs just under the head's surface, easing to its plate over
  3 mm past the margin, whichever way it points; over the thorax it is raised clear of it, as before.
  The head's own outline no longer widens to the spine tip's x (the old arm stretched it, leaving a ledge along the
  side); without a spine the head is exactly as before (so the instrument's spineless closure head is unchanged).
- `isopod_model/shaped_head.py`: the spine is no longer drawn into the columns; `Shape.piece` and `piece_z` (its
  surface: the rounded ridge as before, rising out of the margin); the diagram's outline is the head unioned with the
  piece; new handles `kink` and `web`.
- `isopod_model/body.py`: `genal_piece_solids()` builds the piece's bands beside the body solid to the bed and over the
  body as a WALL-thick plate clear of the thorax (eased between the two, no hard switch); `genal.py` added to the head
  cache signature.
- `blueprint.py`, `scripts/trilomorph_import.py` (the spine's measured direction now goes to `genal_angle`, any way),
  `scripts/head_check.py`, tests.

## For the paper and the Dell

- **Fits.** The 20- and 22-key fits do not fit the spine's direction; landmark 16 sits where the preset's spine puts it,
  and the converted presets move it by ≤ 1.1 mm. Adding `genalAngle` (and `genalTip`) to the fit keys is now possible.
  The flexibility control must be rerun on whatever key set the paper uses.
- **Schema numbers.** The Dell is at 6.6 (sutureFront). This is 6.7 on top of `main` 6.5: merging means the 6.6
  sutureFront keys plus these; neither touches the other's keys.
- **The library** does not need regenerating: its animals convert on load (same spine to within the numbers above).
- Not pushed.

## Known limits

- A web whose edge would cross the spine (a spine aimed forward with a web from the rear margin) is left off with a
  note in the studio.
- A spine aimed in over the body is lifted clear of the thorax; with a long kinked spine over the axis the plate climbs
  steeply (it builds, but looks stepped).
- Curved spines are two straight pieces now; a smooth curve is a kink with a small angle change.
