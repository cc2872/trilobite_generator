# CHANGES 2026-09-12 — orphan-tip diagnosis, builder fix, first H2 pilot

## The problem
A 60-animal pre-registered LHS sweep (sweep.py design, seed 7, 6 non-control animals per order) came
back ~70% `invalid`, every order above the PREREG §4 40% gate, and with ZERO `anatomy`/open animals in
60. Every animal that reached a verdict closed (14/14). Taken at face value that kills H2; taken
correctly it means the instrument was censoring the jam region rather than measuring it.

## Three wrong leads (recorded so nobody re-runs them)
1. **Width-capping the wide-joint band** (band = min(band, 2*(w - TIP_KEEP_MM)) on wide joints).
   Broke frozen readings (proetida/coryn severed). Reverted. Wrong DIRECTION — see below.
2. **Sliver false-positive** (bodies() never applied SLIVER_MM3). Real, but SLIVER_MM3 = 1e-3 caught
   nothing; measured ghost volumes are 0.001–1 mm3. Re-set to 2.0 (in the measured gap). Frozen
   anchors identical. Changed ZERO sweep outcomes — the censoring pieces are 7–68 mm3, real tips.
3. **spineSweep × tipTaper** as the trigger. 17-cell grid on proetida: clean everywhere. They were
   pinned at 45/0.8 in every sweep row (never varied) — a confound, not a cause.

## Root cause (proven)
All three leads inspected segments at the DEFAULT bevel (P["maxAngle"]). The instrument does not
build there: probe_bound steps 45→35→25 and censors at whatever it lands on. Built at the probed
bevel, the picture is clean:
- raw height-field shell: 1 body. front hinge: clean. **rear hinge on the last segment: 3 bodies.**
- the detached tip volume is IDENTICAL at 45/35/25 deg (68.5/68.5 on asaphida seg10). Bevel-
  independent ⇒ not a cut.
- The wide-joint wedge on the last segment is sized off GLOBAL width. On a tapered last segment it
  stops short of the plate edge and leaves the outboard pleural tip as a disconnected orphan
  island — never cut, just stranded. Symmetric pair (one per side), hence the equal-volume pairs.
- Confirmation of direction: capping the band SHORTER made the stranded tip BIGGER (68→20 mm3).
  Widening it to span the full local plate → 1 body, main volume unchanged (800.8).
- Invisible on all 10 hand-tuned presets (last segments happen to be wide enough); near-universal
  once a sweep tapers the rear — i.e. exactly what a morphospace sweep does.

## The fix (parts.py)
`_hinge_geometry(..., overspan=False)`; on the last-segment rear joint only,
`band = max(band, 2*halfwidth + WIDE_OVERSPAN_MM)` (WIDE_OVERSPAN_MM = 4.0). Applied via
`overspan=S["last"]` at the segment() caller. Head and pygidium NOT overspanned: doing so regressed
proetida/corynexochida (caught by the frozen gate; scoped out).

Frozen anchors after the fix: proetida 22.81, corynexochida 22.66, harpetida 30.39 — identical.
