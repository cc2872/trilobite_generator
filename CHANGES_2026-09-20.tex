\section{The instrument: one change}
\code{classify()} implemented ``closed with total \(<150^\circ\) \(\Rightarrow\) discoidal''. PREREG \S3 says discoidal requires \emph{both} the short curl \emph{and} pygidium--cephalon overlap \(s_{\mathrm{tail}}\ge\)~\code{DISC\_OVERLAP} (resolved: 0.5). Restored verbatim; short-curl closures without the overlap now fall through to the \(s_{\mathrm{tail}}\) rules. The constants (\code{DISC\_DEG}, \code{DISC\_OVERLAP}, \code{CLASS\_TOL}) are now written into every result. Agnostida stays discoidal (\(s_{\mathrm{tail}}=1.56\)); no frozen reference changes. This closes the last ``still open'' item in the changelog.

\section{The print joint (\code{printjoint2.py})}
Two Thingiverse print-in-place trilobites were taken apart in code (\#7112261: 31 bodies, T-tenons in loose pockets; \#3839472: 8 bodies, one knob-in-pocket joint per pair). The second is the one adopted, parametrized on our segment geometry:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item every segment is a \emph{solid wedge on a flat base} (no hollow shell, no nesting, no supports);
\item one joint per pair at the midline: a short \emph{barrel} (axis across the body) on a neck, reaching back from segment \(k\) into a box pocket inside segment \(k{+}1\) through a slot in its front face. A barrel, not a disc or sphere, because its fore--aft extent does not change with flexion: pull play equals the axial gap (0.10--0.25\,mm) instead of the 0.6\,mm the Thingiverse disc needs;
\item V-cut faces below the pivot set the stop at \code{maxAngle}; measured stop \(=\)\code{maxAngle}\(\pm2^\circ\) on every slider case and every preset;
\item three clearances by print direction (axial 0.20, vertical 0.30, lateral 0.25\,mm), a 1.4\,mm lip, a 45\(^\circ\) bed chamfer;
\item the pivot is an absolute height (6--10\,mm above the bed), not a fraction of relief; the joint scales itself down when the pitch is within 1\,mm of what it needs and refuses below 4.5\,mm pitch.
\end{itemize}
Whole animals: \code{print\_head}, \code{print\_segment}, \code{print\_tail}, \code{print\_animal}; posed with \code{printjoint2.transforms\_deg} (the print pivot sits \(\approx2.4\)\,mm behind the instrument's joint plane --- state it, do not pose print parts with the instrument's transforms). All ten presets: every part one watertight body, no contact at the stop, including head--tail. Retention: the barrel's 0.65\,mm side shoulders and 0.50\,mm top/bottom shoulders bear on the lip; overlap volume rises steadily past the 0.2\,mm play.

\emph{Known limits.} A ventral curl on solid segments costs base width: at \code{maxAngle}\,36\(^\circ\) the V cut leaves 43\,\% of the pitch on the bed (72\,\% at 18\(^\circ\)). The presets carry \code{maxAngle}\,18, so printed presets make a C, not a ball; the export should take its stop from the instrument's reading for that animal.

\section{Print fill (\code{printfill.py})}
Printed pin-build animals are the 2\,mm measurement shell and are too thin to hold. \code{thicken()} adds the layer between the dorsal surface and the same surface shifted down \code{fill} mm. Hinge bands (barrels, bores, knuckles), the flap that rides over the next segment, and the pleural tips (depth \(\le\) \code{marginHeight}\(\cdot\)\code{relief}\(-1.5\), never below \(z=0.6\)) are kept clear. Default animal at 3\,mm: 26\,g \(\to\) 32\,g, single bodies, no rest contact, clean to its 18\(^\circ\) stop. Three mm is the ceiling for the pin build (the shells nest at 2.3\,mm). The measured geometry and the parameter hash are untouched: the sheet still describes the 2\,mm animal.

\section{Web}
\code{/api/build} body may carry \code{"joint": "pin"|"flexi"} (default pin) and \code{"fill": <mm>} (pin only). Cache keys get \code{-flexi} / \code{-fill3} suffixes so measured and print builds never collide; the manifest records \code{joint}, \code{fill\_mm} and, for flexi, a \code{print\_joint} block (pivot, offset, scale, gaps). \code{/api/stl} poses flexi builds with the print pivot. \code{index.html} is unchanged --- no toggle yet; one checkbox beside \emph{Download STL} is all it needs.
