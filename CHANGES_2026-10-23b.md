# Changes, 23 October 2026 (b): curved spines, schema 6.5

Untested on the printable animal. The pin head is tested; the isopod model builds are the Dell's job
(`scripts/build_ornaments.py`, below). Nothing here touches the instrument's path: `joints/`, `instrument.py` and
the thorax plan are unchanged, and every new key defaults to 0, where every spine is built exactly as before.

## Why
The 23 Oct list of 50 genera (Weaver's email) has about ten the generator could not make at all: recurved horns
(*Dicranurus*, *Ceratarges*, *Boedaspis*, *Miraspis*), forked occipital spines, frontal processes (*Odontocephalus*,
*Pagetia*, *Yukonia*). Every grafted spine was a straight tapered frustum placed by yaw and pitch; a horn that curls
over itself cannot be one. The trident (*Walliserops*) was never lost: `anatomy.common.prong()` and the six
`headProng*` keys are in the tree, and the shaped head already grafts it. What was missing is curvature.

## What changed
* `anatomy/common.py`: `curved_spine(base_r, tip_r, length, at, yaw, pitch, bend_deg, roll_deg)`: the same taper
  laid along a circular arc that turns `bend_deg` from root to tip, in a plane rolled `roll_deg` about the spine's
  own axis (0 up, 90 out, 180 down); n frustums joined by spheres, one closed solid. `bend_deg = 0` returns
  `spine_solid()` byte for byte. `spine_tip()` gives the tip's position (for clearances and the sheet). `prong()`
  takes `bend_deg` / `roll_deg` and arches each tine.
* `schema.py` 6.5, twelve keys, all default 0 (or 1 tine): `occipitalBend`, `occipitalTines` (1-3), `occipitalSplay`,
  `genalHorn` (a solid spine from each genal angle / head length), `genalHornPitch`, `genalHornBend`, `genalHornRoll`,
  `frontSpine` (a median spine from the front margin), `frontSpineBend`, `termBend`, `headProngBend`, `tailProngBend`.
  Placed in CELLS b1 / a1 / b3 / a3 beside the keys they extend.
* `anatomy/head.py`: `spine_ornaments()`, one function that builds the head's grafted spines (occipital with tines and
  bend, prong with bend, frontal spine, genal horns), called by the pin head's `ornaments()` AND by both isopod-model
  head paths (`isopod_model/body.py`: `isopod_head`, `shaped_head`), which had their own copies of the occipital
  spine and the prong. The plan now returns `y_genal` (the genal angle's y).
* `anatomy/tail.py`, `isopod_model/body.py`: the terminal spine and the tail prong take `termBend` / `tailProngBend`.
* `tests/test_curved_spines.py` (16 tests): the primitive, arc length, the arched trident, the defaults as a no-op,
  five ornament sets on the pin head as one body. `tests/test_schema.py` pins 6.5.
* `scripts/build_ornaments.py`: builds the default animal and five ornamented animals on the isopod model.

## Not done
* **The pleural spines and the pin head's genal spines cannot bend.** Both are part of the height field (the plan's
  outline), not grafted solids. Bending them out of the plane means a second surface; the shaped head's in-plane
  `genal_curve` is what exists. `genalHorn` is the solid alternative for a horn that rises off the shield.
* **No overhang pass on the isopod model.** A horn that sweeps back over seg0 is cleared the way every head feature is
  (the posed segments swept through the dilated head), so it will be cut where seg0 passes. Long genal spines over
  several segments (*Olenellus*) are the same open item as before.
* **The studio does not draw the new spines** (`head_diagrams.py`, `shaped_head.diagram`). Builds and STLs are right;
  the plan and elevation on the site do not show them yet.
* **Print validity**: a tip of 0.45 mm model radius is under a nozzle at small print scales. No rule yet; the kit's
  `holochroal` lens rule is the pattern to follow.

## To run on the Dell
    py -m pytest tests\test_curved_spines.py tests\test_schema.py -q        (~3 min)
    py scripts\build_ornaments.py C:\trilib\ornaments                       (~15 min; STLs in C:\trilib\ornaments\<name>\)
Then print one: `ceratarges` or `dicranurus` is the test of whether a recurved horn survives the slicer and a drop.
