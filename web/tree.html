Sources

What the generator is built from, what it is read against, and the data the Earth layout shows. Page numbers refer to the edition cited.

AppliedIn the generator: used directly in the shapes, the ruler or the export.

Gon, S. M. III. 2009. A Pictorial Guide to the Orders of Trilobites. In the generator: the ten order presets and the 24 head styles (pp. 52-84, page per style); facial sutures, proparian / gonatoparian / opisthoparian (pp. 21, 26); eye ridges (p. 41); cyclopygid eyes (p. 44); the enrolment definitions and coaptation (pp. 31-32).

Esteve, J., Rubio, P., Zamora, S. & Rahman, I. A. 2017. Modelling enrolment in Cambrian trilobites. Palaeontology 60: 423-432. doi:10.1111/pala.12294. Data: Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.s29j7. In the generator: the 150° split between enrolment classes; the per-joint bend profile (45° at the head joint, logarithmic decay) in the exploratory instrument; the Bailiaspis and Parabailella models as comparison animals; the 14- vs 15-segment closure case.

Esteve, J. & Hughes, N. C. 2023. Developmental and functional controls on enrolment in an ancient, extinct arthropod. Proc. R. Soc. B 290: 20230871. In the generator: the segment-count sweep (the sphaeroidal to external-spiral switch near 18 segments in Aulacopleura) as a pre-registered test.

Bergström, J. 1973. Organization, life, and systematics of trilobites. Fossils and Strata 2: 1-69. In the generator: the enrolment class names sphaeroidal, double (internal spiral) and spiral (external spiral), as relayed by Whittington 1996 and Esteve et al. 2017. Original not yet consulted.

Whittington, H. B. 1996. Olenellid trilobites: the thorax and enrolment. Palaeontology 39: 377-388. In the generator: the articulation set (fulcrum, articulating half ring, facet) the thorax plan carries; the cylindrical type the instrument cannot yet score.

Whittington, H. B. et al. 1997. Treatise on Invertebrate Paleontology, Part O, Trilobita, revised, vol. 1. Clarkson, E. N. K. & Whittington, H. B., Enrollment and coaptative structures, pp. 67-73. In the generator: the segment vocabulary (axial ring, half ring, furrows, pleurae, facet, spine) used by the single-segment program and the sheet.

Bruton, D. L. & Haas, W. 1997. Functional morphology of Phacopinae (Trilobita) and the mechanics of enrolment. Palaeontographica A 245: 1-43. In the generator: the rotation-axis convention (anterior pleural edge under the preceding segment) the instrument's pin hinge is compared against.

Raup, D. M. 1966. Geometric analysis of shell coiling: general problems. J. Paleontology 40: 1178-1190. In the generator: the morphospace framing; the few-axis dials and the sweep design.

Serra, F., Balseiro, D., Monnet, C., Randolfe, E., Bignon, A., Rustán, J. J., Bault, V., Muñoz, D. F., Vaccari, N. E., Martinetto, M., Crônier, C. & Waisfeld, B. G. 2023. A dynamic and collaborative database for morphogeometric information of trilobites. Scientific Data 10: 841. doi:10.1038/s41597-023-02724-9. TriloMorph, stable release doi:10.5281/zenodo.8033445 (CC BY 4.0; contributors listed in its contributors.csv). In the generator: the landmark protocol (16 cephalic and 7 pygidial landmarks, 4 + 3 semilandmark curves) that landmarks.py reads off the head and tail plans and the TPS export writes, so a generated animal and a photographed one share one Procrustes superimposition; the 5-95 % ranges of their cephala and pygidia set axisFrac, genalSpine, preglabField and the eyeLat default (schema 6.3).

Rohlf, F. J. tpsDig and the TPS file format; Adams, D. C., Collyer, M. L. & Kaliontzopoulou, A. geomorph (R). In the generator: the TPS export (landmarks.py), for Procrustes analysis in geomorph alongside TriloMorph.
