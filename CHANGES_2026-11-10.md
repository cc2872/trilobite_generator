# 10 Nov 2026 — hand-sized prints, joints that follow the animal

## Why
The ten order presets exported 223–596 mm long (8 of 10 past a 256 mm bed, 1–2.8 L of plastic). Every animal was built at the
isopod's own 18 mm pitch per segment, and the joint kit was fixed at `jointScale` (0.6) of the isopod's whatever the animal's
size. The isopod model scaled down whole, to 1/2 and 1/3, still prints and articulates (Claire, 10 Nov), so the joints can
shrink with the animal.

## What changed
- **`printLength` 0 (the default) = hand size** (`isopod_model/body.py`: `HAND_SIZE`, `HAND_MM` = 160). The whole animal is
  scaled so its longest side in plan is 160 mm. The joints keep their size (`jointScale`, 0.6 of the isopod's) while the
  shortest thoracic segment still holds them. Below that they shrink with the segments, and the gaps between the pieces
  shrink with them. They never go below `KIT_MIN` = 1/3 of the isopod's (ball 3.8 mm, ball-socket clearance 0.08 mm, the
  size the isopod itself was printed at). Where that floor binds, the animal stays as big as the floor needs, and the
  manifest says so.
- The build measures itself. The first build uses a guess, its plan extent gives the size at the isopod's pitch, and if
  the guess was more than 4 % off the animal is built once more at size. The extent is remembered per animal, so the full
  build after a preview goes straight to size.
- An explicit `printLength` (80–600) keeps the old rule: the body head to tail, joints at `jointScale`, constant gaps.
  A body spec's own `print_scale` is kept, and its joints follow it.
- `rows.plans(..., kit=)`: the joint kit can be given (the hand-sized build passes the one it chose).
- `web/app.py`: `_iso_problems` checks the hand-sized print at its smallest; the manifest's print notes carry the print size and
  the joints (`kit`, ball, clearance); `kinematics.kit` carries the kit for the sheet.
- `blueprint.py`: Detail B and the info box draw the ball and clearance of the kit as printed (they showed the 1:1 ball,
  11.5 mm, and the pin hinge's `clearance`, 0.30, on every animal). The bed layout puts the whole animal on the bed: it
  prints in place (captive balls), so the old per-piece packing onto several plates could not be assembled.
- `schema.py`: the `printLength` and `jointScale` docs (values and defaults unchanged, so no parameter hash changes).
- `tests/test_hand_size.py`: the size and kit rules (fast), and the agnostid built hand-sized (slow).

## Not changed
- The head joint is the tightest at every size: the lichid's head-to-seg0 gap is about 0.001 mm at one spot at 1/3 (it
  was 0.09 at full size), the agnostid has one 0.03 mm spot where the tail meets seg1. The thinnest plates also scale: the
  lichid at its floor has 3.5 % of its surface under 0.4 mm.
- The J1 socket pinch on the head (non-manifold edges where the head's rear port meets the first segment's ball) and the
  lichid's tail mesh are still open.
- The instrument (pin builds) is untouched.

## Later the same day: phacopida, odontopleurida, redlichiida
- **Nothing below the bed** (`body.cut_at_bed`, at the end of every build): a spine's root or a tail spine rolled down reached
  under z = 0 on the phacopid (0.5 mm), odontopleurid (0.5), redlichiid (1.3) and lichid (0.6) tails at hand size, and a
  slicer then lifted every other piece off the plate. What is below the bed is cut off; the manifest notes how much.
- **The odontopleurid prints hand-sized**: its drawing block's pinned `print_scale` 0.8 is removed (`presets/odontopleurida.json`,
  `saved_at` bumped so the live box re-mirrors the saved body). Now 165 × 132 × 18 mm (was 231 × 191), joints 0.39 of the isopod's.
- The redlichiid's "0.12 mm spine tips" were not spine tips: a row of sub-resolution teeth (0.05-0.25 mm) along each segment's
  rear edge, left by the clearance cut. They are below any printer's resolution; nothing changed for them.
