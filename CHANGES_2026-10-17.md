# Changes, 17 October 2026

## Studio: a build belongs to one animal; editing invalidates it (17 Oct, polish)

In the studio, BUILD produces an STL download link and an embedded 3D view, both tied to the exact
animal that was built. Before this change, clicking "← editor", tweaking a slider or handle, and then
downloading the STL or toggling to VIEW 3D silently gave the **pre-edit** animal:

- the `download STL` link in the links row persisted across edits (pointed at the old mesh key);
- the VIEW 3D iframe only reset its `src` when the preset/head string changed, so an edit-and-rebuild
  of the same preset kept showing the stale mesh (the iframe never re-read the fresh draft).

Now a built animal is tracked by its build key (`S.built`). Any edit - a row, a head key, the P-eye,
or a prong - calls `invalidateBuild()`, which drops the download link and shows
`edited - rebuild to download / view 3D`. Loading a new preset/body clears it too. The VIEW 3D embed
carries the build key as `&sig=`, so a rebuilt animal always reloads the iframe onto the current draft
instead of the previous mesh. The STL you download and the 3D you view now always match what you last built.

## Tree page: a Sources overlay (17 Oct)

The tree page (`web/tree.html`) gets a **Sources** button in the top HUD, alongside Play. It opens a
scrollable bibliography over the stage - the literature the generator is built from and read against,
split into Applied (used directly in the shapes, the ruler or the export, each with its "In the generator:"
note), Reference (enrolment; anatomy, development and affinities; three-dimensional specimens; theoretical
morphology) and Assets (the print models and the software). The three DOIs provided link out; no DOIs were
invented for the entries that lacked them. Styled in the page's own mono/dark idiom; open with the button or
`S`, close with the button or `Escape`; while open it is modal (the layout and time keys are swallowed).
Author diacritics are kept; page ranges use hyphens and the degree sign stays, matching the site's house style.

## The enrolment bend profile, the head joint, and where the axis is

Print model, sheet and site only. instrument.py, joints/pin.py, tests/references and PREREG are unchanged
(tests/test_kinematics.py asserts the instrument still bends uniformly).

### kinematics.py: Esteve et al. 2017's expression
theta(i) = Ln[a i] b for thoracic joint i = 1, 2, ... (Esteve, Rubio, Zamora & Rahman 2017, Palaeontology 60; restated in
Esteve & Hughes 2023, Proc R Soc B 290, Methods). Fitted to their Fig. 5A (spiral model, 15 segments): a = 0.00194,
b = -3.686 deg, so the first thoracic joint is c = b ln a = 23.0 deg and the curve falls by 3.686 deg per e-fold of joint
number; largest residual against the fourteen angles read off the figure 0.05 deg; 14 c + b ln(14!) + 45 = 274 deg, their total.
The cephalon joint is HEAD_DEG = 45 (set from their fossils, not on the curve). `angles(n, head_deg)` scales the whole
profile so the head joint is at head_deg: that scalar is what the reading sweeps, the sheet draws and the viewer's slider sets.
Not represented: their sphaeroidal curve (21.8 -> 3.3 deg over 13 joints), whose drop accelerates towards the tail and does not
follow this form as read; their a, b per style are in the 2023 paper's table S1 (not seen).

Where it is used: isopod_model/enroll.py (the print model's reading: theta_joint_deg is now the HEAD joint's angle; the row
carries angles_deg and total_deg), blueprint.isopod_sheet (ENROLLED, DETAIL B at the joint's own angle, the five ENROLMENT
frames), web/app.py (kinematics.profile with the build), web/index.html (per-joint rotation in the 3D view; the slider reads
"deg at the head - total"). Clearances in body.py stay UNIFORM at CURL: the printed joint moves on its own, so every joint is
cleared to what the kit can do; the profile is how the animal is posed, not what the object permits.

### body.py: the cephalon joint relieved to 50 deg (HEAD_JOINT_DEG)
Measured on the default animal: the kit stops every joint at 36 deg (0.99 mm3 at 36, 14.7 at 45), the foot of the neck of
the piece behind swinging into the column base of the piece ahead, at the bed, |x| < 3.5 mm, 2-3 mm ahead of the pivot.
`relieve_head_joint` cuts from the HEAD only the volume seg0 sweeps through when J1 turns from CURL to HEAD_JOINT_DEG with the
other joints at rest (1 deg steps, Manifold union of the intersections): about 30 mm3, nothing at or below CURL, seg0 untouched.
After: J1 clean to 50 deg; the profile sweep on the default animal reaches head 48 deg (219 deg total) before J1 touches.

### The segment count (measured, not changed)
Same kit, same profile, textured preset with segCount set, head angle swept until interference or a head-tail gap < 3 mm:
  15 thoracic segments: closes at head 43-45 deg, ~275 deg total  (Esteve: Bailiaspis, 15 segments, 44.6 deg, 274 deg)
  14: closes at 46-48 deg, ~280 deg (Esteve's Parabailella closes at 229 on their faster-decaying sphaeroidal curve)
  12: tail touches the cephalon at 51 deg with a 7.8 mm midline gap
   9 (the default): open at the 50 deg relief limit, 40 mm gap
So an Esteve-length body enrols at Esteve's head angle to Esteve's total on the print kit; a nine-segment body does not close
with this profile. That is their 15-vs-14 mechanism, seen on our hardware.

### kinematics.dorsal_pivots: Esteve's axis against the kit's
Their axis is at the anterior edge of each pleura under the plate ahead: dorsal, at the overlap. Ours is the ball centre:
ventral, 7.5 mm up. `dorsal_pivots(pieces)` takes the rear edge of each piece's plate on the midline (underside). Same
angles about that axis: the 15-segment body is closed by head 34-36 deg (~230 deg, their sphaeroidal total) and the
nine-segment default closes at 48 deg. The pivot alone accounts for about 10 deg at the head. The sheet's ENROLLED panel draws
the dorsal-axis pose in grey over the ball-axis pose; `enroll.reading(..., pivot="dorsal")` gives the comparison reading, marked
as not a print reading (the kit cannot take that pose: the dorsal pose is the fossils' mechanism on our dorsal geometry).

### web/app.py: the site password, and the cache routes' key check
TRILO_SITE_PASSWORD set => every page redirects to /login and every /api/* and /files/* call answers 401 until the signed
cookie is there (30 days; HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax; signed with TRILO_SECRET_KEY, or a per-process key if unset, in which case
a restart logs everyone out). /login is the site's own look (black, one hairline input); a wrong password waits half a second
and answers 401; `next` is kept to same-site paths. /logout clears it. Programs: on /api/agent/* and /llms.txt the agent
token (?token= or Bearer) stands in for the cookie. Unset => the site is open and the log says so. Both settings are in
.env.example (values blank); the password itself is not in the repo.
Also: /files/<key>/<name> and /api/stl/<key> now refuse anything but a cache key `[0-9a-f]{10}(-[A-Za-z0-9.]+)*` and a plain
file name (404), closing the path traversal that served web/agent_token.txt (review, 9 and 13 Oct); /api/stl answers 404 for
an unknown key instead of a traceback.
Tests: tests/test_site_password.py (5). Web suites with it: 40 passed.
