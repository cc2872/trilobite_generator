# 26 Sep 2026: flexi builds now match the pin build's anatomy

## The bug
On 06f24ab3bc the flexi STL was 87.0 mm wide and the pin STL 121.5 mm. The flexi joint trims every part to one
pitch (segments) or to the joint plane (head). Anything that reaches BACK past that line was deleted: pleural
spines, falcate pleural tips, the genal arms (replaced by a generic stand-in horn), and the head's rear flap.

## Also found
The head's occipital spine sweeps back through seg0's axial spine. At rest that is 76 mm3 of solid overlap in the
flexi build: the old flexi print of this animal would have been welded at the first joint. The pin build has the
same collision at 3.6 mm3, over the instrument's 1.0 mm3 rest budget, so the instrument will read this animal as
invalid (rest_interference). That is a property of the parameters, not of the print code.

## The fix (printjoint2.py, print only; parts.py / mesh.py / instrument.py untouched, no reading can move)
restore(): puts the trimmed anatomy back from the pin build's own plans, then subtracts from it every later part
posed through the curl (-2 deg to maxAngle, joints together and one at a time), grown by the gaps. Called by
print_animal() and by the site (web/app.py). The occipital spine goes through the same step. J['overhangs']=False
gives the old behaviour.

Result on 06f24ab3bc: width 121.5 mm (same as pin), every part one watertight body, 0 mm3 overlap between any
two parts in 10 poses. Build time 7 s -> 25 s.
Print note: genal arms are the pin build's raised band, so they overhang the thorax (2.8 to 7.4 mm of air under
them). Slicer supports under the arms: yes. Pleural spines sit on the bed and need none.

## web/app.py
* flexi builds run restore() after the per-part builds (one extra progress step)
* STL download: each mesh gets ITS part's transform (a failed part used to shift every later part); refuses a
  file with non-finite points or > 600 mm; writes to a temp name and renames, so a half-written file is never served

## tests
* test_printjoint2: new test_overhangs_keep_the_pin_outline_and_clear_the_curl
* test_web: schema version and parameter count now read from schema.py (were hard-coded 6.0 / 95)


# 26 Sep 2026 (later): parts, joints, and the one place they meet

## Why
The joint was spread over six files (parts.py cut the pin into each part while building the anatomy, the instrument
set its bevel and bent the chain, printjoint2.py had a second joint and its own bending, printfill.py kept clear of
the pin, the site and the blueprint read hinge numbers directly). The head was written twice (parts.cephalon and
printjoint2.print_head) and the copies drifted: that is where the morning's bug came from.

## The layout
    anatomy/  head.py thorax.py tail.py common.py port.py   the parts, joint-free; a part describes its joint planes as Ports
    joints/   base.py pin.py flexi.py                        every joint on one template; pin is the measured one
    assemble.py                                              plate -> joint at every port -> ornaments; the overhang pass
parts.py and printjoint2.py stay as thin compatibility names. instrument.py, web/app.py, printfill.py and blueprint.py
call assemble / joints / anatomy. The flat originals are in legacy/ (parts_v6_flat.py, printjoint2_v3_flat.py).

## Checks
* pin parts: identical geometry to the flat builder (volume to 1e-3, bounds to 1e-3, two-way surface distance 0.0000 mm)
  on proetida and 06f24ab3bc at bevel 45 and at the printed stop, every part type. Manifold re-triangulates the same
  solid differently depending on how many objects it has seen, so face counts can differ; the geometry does not.
* flexi parts: exact boolean difference against the 26 Sep fixed build of 06f24ab3bc is zero on all 11 parts.
* the whole existing test suite passes unchanged (it imports the old names), including the frozen references.
* new: tests/test_joint_contract.py (both joints), tests/test_head_contract.py.

## Rules the layout enforces
* a joint reads a Port and the parameter table; never a part's plan
* a part never knows a joint exists; its ornaments are unioned after the joint by the assembler
* only assemble.py imports both
* joints/pin.py is MEASURED: a change there is an instrument change (new version, re-freeze references)
